Project Description
Azure Power Rooftop (GenCo
.
) Private Limited (‘Azure
roof” or the “Company”) is a 100% subsidiary owned by IFC’s existing client Azure Power Global Limited (Azure or the Sponsor). Azure is a leading solar power company in India with a track record of robust execution and strong growth. Azure has in-house capacity to undertake engineering, procurement & construction (EPC) as well as operations & maintenance (O&M) services for all its solar power plants.
Sponsor / Cost / Location
Project Sponsor and Major Shareholders of Project Company
Azure Power Global Limited (Azure or the Sponsor) is a leading solar power company in India and the only
Indian independent power producer to be listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE). As of November
2017, Azure has a well-diversified portfolio of around 1.6GW committed and operating solar projects in 20+
states across India. Of these, 48 are operational projects and 20 are under construction or committed projects.
Total Project Cost and Amount and Nature of IFC's Investment
The proposed IFC investment involves a debt financing of up to US$45 million to the Company and mobilization of senior loans
of up to US$90 million

Location of Project and Description of Site
The proceeds of the IFC investment will be used to fund the construction of green field solar projects across
India.
Development Impact
Anticipated Impact Measurement & Monitoring (AIMM) Assessment
1. Increasing clean energy production: The Project will contribute to meeting the country's energy demand by using an environmentally friendly source of energy.
2. Climate change mitigation: IFC's investment will support clean energy production with associated avoidance of GHG emissions.
IFC's Role and Additionality
1. Mobilization: IFC's investment will help mobilize funding for the Company's expansion plans.
2. Stamp of Approval: IFC's participation in the Project helps affirm the viability of the rooftop solar sector in India and seeks to promote greater private sector investment in distributed solar power development.
E&S Category Rationale / Risks and Mitigation
Environmental & Social Categorization Rationale
This is a Category B project per IFC’s Sustainability Policy as the E&S risks and impacts associated with the project are limited, site-specific and could be addressed through the implementation of standard good international industry practices, design guidelines and design criteria.
The key E&S risks identified through the project life cycle include: occupational health and safety risks for workers; procurement and management of human resources including contract workers; waste management; infrastructural design and equipment safety in public buildings such as railway stations; and structural safety of buildings onto which rooftop panels will be installed.
